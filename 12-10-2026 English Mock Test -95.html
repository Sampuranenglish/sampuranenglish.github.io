Innocuous (Adjective)
Meaning: not harmful or offensive; not likely to cause any damage or annoy anyone
Ex – His remark seemed innocuous at first, but it hurt her deeply.
Synonyms: harmless, safe, benign, inoffensive, innocent
Antonyms: harmful, dangerous, injurious, toxic, noxious
