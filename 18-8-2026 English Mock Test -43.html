Rule of the Day
When two or more adjectives are used before a noun, they follow a fixed order: Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Colour → Origin → Material → Purpose. Ex – She bought a plastic small red bag. (Incorrect) Ex – She bought a small red plastic bag. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Deference (Noun)
Meaning: humble submission and respect shown towards someone, often by yielding to their wishes or judgement
Ex – The young lawyer spoke in deference to the senior judge's ruling.
Synonyms: respect, reverence, regard, esteem, obeisance
Antonyms: disrespect, disregard, defiance, impoliteness, contempt
