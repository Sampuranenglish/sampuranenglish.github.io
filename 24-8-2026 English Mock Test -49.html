Rule of the Day
Pronoun Rule: A pronoun used for a collective noun (e.g., team, family, jury, committee) should be singular ("it") when the group acts as one unit, but plural ("they") when the members act individually. Ex- The jury has given their verdict. (Incorrect) Ex- The jury has given its verdict. (Correct — acting as one unit)
Word of the Day
Restive (Adjective)
Meaning: unable to keep still or stay quiet; impatient or restless because of dissatisfaction, delay, or a desire for change
Ex- The crowd grew restive as the concert's start was delayed by over an hour.
Synonyms: restless, fidgety, impatient, uneasy
Antonyms: calm, docile, patient
