Rule of the Day
Use "fewer" with countable plural nouns and "less" with uncountable nouns. Ex – There are less students this year. (Incorrect) Ex – There are fewer students this year. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Vivacious (Adjective)
Meaning: attractively lively and animated
Ex – Her vivacious personality made her the centre of attention at every gathering.
Synonyms: lively, spirited, energetic, animated
Antonyms: dull, lethargic, listless
