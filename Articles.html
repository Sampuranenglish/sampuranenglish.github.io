Chapter: Articles

RULE - 35
A / An depends on SOUND, not spelling
Vowel sound = an | Consonant sound = a
  • Ex- She waited for an hour.
  • Ex- He is an honest man.
  • Ex- He studies in a university.
  • Ex- A one-rupee note is on the table.
  • Ex- Ex- He is an engineer.
  • Ex- Ex- There is a European person in the shop.
RULE - 36
Such / What / Quite / Rather + a / an + Noun
  • Ex- What a beautiful day!
  • Ex- He is such an honest man.
RULE - 37
The + Unique Thing
(sun, moon, earth, sky, world)
  • Ex- The sun rises in the east.
  • Ex- The moon shines at night.
RULE - 38
The + Superlative
The + Ordinal (first, second, last)
  • Ex- She is the best student in the class.
  • Ex- He was the first to arrive.
RULE - 39
The + Rivers, Seas, Oceans, Mountain Ranges, Deserts, Holy Books, Newspapers
  • Ex- The Ganga is a holy river.
  • Ex- The Himalayas are in the north.
  • Ex- He reads The Hindu daily.
  • Note: We do not use the definite article 'the' before specific or individual mountain peaks (Mount Everest, Mount Abu, Mount Kilimanjaro, etc.). We use the article 'the' before mountain ranges (The Himalayas, The Alps, The Aravalli Range, etc.)
RULE - 40
No article before Persons, Cities, Countries, Continents
Exception: the USA, the UK, the Netherlands
  • Ex- Delhi is the capital of India. (Correct)
  • Ex- The India is a big country. (Wrong)
RULE - 41
No article before Languages, Meals, Games, Subjects
  • Ex- She speaks Hindi.
  • Ex- We play cricket after lunch.
  • Ex- He is weak in Mathematics.
RULE - 42
Play + the + Musical Instrument
Play + Game (no article)
  • Ex- She plays the guitar. (Correct)
  • Ex- He plays cricket. (Correct)
  • Ex- He plays the cricket. (Wrong)
RULE - 43
School / College / Prison / Bed / Church
No 'the' when used for the main purpose
  • Ex- He goes to school every day. (to study)
  • Ex- He went to the school to meet the principal. (visit)
  • Ex- The thief was sent to prison.
RULE - 44
A + Noun + and + Noun = One Person
A + Noun + and + A + Noun = Two Persons
  • Ex- A poet and novelist is coming. (one person)
  • Ex- A poet and a novelist are coming. (two persons)
RULE - 45
First mention = a / an
Second mention = the
  • Ex- I saw a boy. The boy was crying.
  • Ex- There was a crow. The crow was very thirsty.
RULE - 46
Always the + same / only / following
  • Ex- We study in the same class.
  • Ex- He is the only son of his parents.
  • Ex- Read the following passage.
