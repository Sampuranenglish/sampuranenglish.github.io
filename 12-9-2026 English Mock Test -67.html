Rule of the Day
(Chapter: Pronoun) — A reflexive pronoun (myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves) is used only when the subject and the object of a verb refer to the same person or thing. It must NEVER be used as the subject of a sentence, nor in place of a simple personal pronoun (I, he, she, we) when another person is also doing the action. Ex – Myself and Rohan will attend the seminar tomorrow. (Incorrect) Ex – Rohan and I will attend the seminar tomorrow. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Disparity (Noun)
Meaning: a great difference or lack of equality between two or more things, especially in a way that seems unfair
Ex – The report highlighted the widening disparity in income between urban and rural households.
Synonyms: inequality, discrepancy, imbalance
Antonyms: parity, equality, similarity
