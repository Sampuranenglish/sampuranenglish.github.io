Rule of the Day
When two elements are joined by correlative conjunctions (not only...but also, either...or, neither...nor), both elements must be grammatically parallel. Ex – She not only sings but also she dances beautifully. (Incorrect) Ex – She not only sings but also dances beautifully. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Exemplary (Adjective)
Meaning: serving as a desirable model; outstanding
Ex – Her exemplary conduct during the crisis earned her a promotion.
Synonyms: model, ideal, commendable, outstanding
Antonyms: deplorable, poor, substandard
