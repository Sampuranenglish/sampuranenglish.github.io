Word of the Day
Exorbitant (Adjective)
Meaning: (of a price or demand) unreasonably high; far more than is fair or normal
Ex – The hotel charged an exorbitant fee for a single night's stay.
Synonyms: excessive, steep, inflated, extortionate, unconscionable
Antonyms: reasonable, moderate, affordable, cheap, nominal
