Rule of the Day
(Chapter: Conditional Tense) — In a Type 1 (real/possible) conditional sentence, the "if-clause" takes the Present Simple Tense, while the main clause takes the Future Simple Tense ("will" + V1). We must not use "will" inside the if-clause itself. Ex – If it will rain tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic. (Incorrect) Ex – If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Fatuous (Adjective)
Meaning: silly and pointless; showing a complete lack of thought or judgment
Ex – The board dismissed his fatuous suggestion to cut costs by switching off the office lights during working hours.
Synonyms: inane, vacuous, foolish
Antonyms: sensible, judicious, prudent
