Rule of the Day
(Chapter: Tense) — When a sentence begins with "It is (high) time", the clause that follows is written in the Past Simple Tense. Ex – It is time, we start preparing for the exam. (Incorrect) Ex – It is time, we started preparing for the exam. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Propensity (Noun)
Meaning: a natural or habitual inclination or tendency to behave in a particular way
Ex – He has a propensity for taking unnecessary risks while driving.
Synonyms: inclination, tendency, penchant
Antonyms: aversion, disinclination, reluctance
