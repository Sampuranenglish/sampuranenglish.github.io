Rule of the Day
Discuss is followed directly by its object. Never add “about”. Ex – We discussed about the plan. (Incorrect) Ex – We discussed the plan. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Impediment (Noun)
Meaning: a hindrance or obstruction that makes it difficult to do or achieve something
Ex – Lack of funds proved a major impediment to the completion of the project.
Synonyms: obstacle, hindrance, barrier, hurdle, obstruction
Antonyms: aid, help, assistance, facilitation
