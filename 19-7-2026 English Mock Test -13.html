Rule of the Day
Use "since" with a fixed point in time (a date, day, or moment) and "for" with a duration or period of time. Ex – I have lived here since five years. (Incorrect) Ex – I have lived here for five years. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Astute (Adjective)
Meaning: having or showing an ability to accurately assess situations and turn this to one's advantage
Ex – Her astute handling of the negotiations secured a favourable deal for the company.
Synonyms: shrewd, perceptive, sharp, canny
Antonyms: naive, gullible, foolish
