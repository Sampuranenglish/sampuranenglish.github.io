Rule of the Day
"Each", "every", "either", and "neither" are indefinite pronouns/determiners that are always followed by a singular verb, even when they refer to a group of people or things. Ex – Each of the boys have finished their homework. (Incorrect) Ex – Each of the boys has finished his homework. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Obsequious (Adjective)
Meaning: excessively eager to please or obey someone; servile and fawning
Ex – The new intern's obsequious behaviour towards the manager made his colleagues uncomfortable.
Synonyms: sycophantic, fawning, servile
Antonyms: assertive, domineering, arrogant
