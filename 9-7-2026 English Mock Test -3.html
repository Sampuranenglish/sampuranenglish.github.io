Rule of the Day
If a gerund (V1 + ing) is preceded by a Pronoun, that Pronoun must be in the Possessive case. Ex – He emphasized me going there. (Incorrect) Ex – He emphasized my going there. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Lethargic (Adjective)
Meaning: having little energy; feeling unwilling and unable to do anything
Ex – I was feeling tired and lethargic.
Trick: Lethargic = जो हर समय लेटा (Leta) रहे
Synonyms: inactive, indolent, inert, torpid, sluggish, languid, laggard
Antonyms: active, alert, animated, energetic, vigorous, vivacious, lively
