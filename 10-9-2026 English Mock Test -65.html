Rule of the Day
(Chapter: Preposition) — "Between" is used when referring to exactly two people, things, or groups. "Among" is used when referring to more than two. Ex – The prize money was divided between the five winners. (Incorrect) Ex – The prize money was divided among the five winners. (Correct) Ex – A friendly rivalry exists between the two neighbouring villages. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ostentatious (Adjective)
Meaning: characterized by vulgar or pretentious display; designed to impress others
Ex – The billionaire's ostentatious mansion, complete with gold-plated fixtures, drew criticism from his more modest neighbours.
Synonyms: showy, flashy, pretentious
Antonyms: modest, understated, unassuming
