Rule of the Day
When two or more singular nouns are joined by or, nor, either...or, neither...nor, the pronoun referring to them must be singular. Words like or and nor indicate choice, not addition. Ex – Either Ram or Shyam will bring their books. (Incorrect) Ex – Either Ram or Shyam will bring his book. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Curtail (Verb)
Meaning: to make something shorter or smaller; to reduce (काटना / कम करना)
Ex – I had to curtail my answer as I was running out of time.
Trick: Curtail → Cut tail. If a dog's tail is cut, it becomes shorter.
Synonyms: cut, reduce, shorten, cut down, diminish, trim, abbreviate, truncate
Antonyms: enlarge, expand, extend, stretch, amplify, prolong
