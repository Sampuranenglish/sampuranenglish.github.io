Rule of the Day
Adjectives like "unique", "perfect", and "complete" express an absolute quality and should never be used in the comparative or superlative degree. Ex – This is the most unique solution to the problem. (Incorrect) Ex – This is a unique solution to the problem. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ebullient (Adjective)
Meaning: cheerful and full of energy; overflowing with enthusiasm
Ex – The team was ebullient after their unexpected victory in the finals.
Synonyms: exuberant, buoyant, effervescent
Antonyms: gloomy, despondent, listless
