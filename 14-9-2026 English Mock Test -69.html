Rule of the Day
(Chapter: Adjective) — Certain adjectives are "absolute" or "non-gradable" (perfect, unique, complete, full, ideal, impossible, etc.) because they already express the highest possible degree of a quality. Such adjectives cannot be used in the comparative or superlative degree, and should never be preceded by intensifiers like "very," "more," or "most." Ex – This is the most unique idea I have ever heard. (Incorrect) Ex – This is a unique idea I have ever heard. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Dearth (Noun)
Meaning: a scarcity or lack of something; an insufficient supply
Ex – There is a dearth of affordable housing in the city centre.
Synonyms: scarcity, shortage, paucity
Antonyms: abundance, plenty, surplus
