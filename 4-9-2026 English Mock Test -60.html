Rule of the Day
(Chapter: Tense) — When "wish" is used to express a desire for something to be different in the present, the verb that follows takes the Past Simple Tense (and "were" is used for all persons with the verb "be"). We do not use the Present Tense after "wish" in such cases. Ex – I wish I know the answer to this question. (Incorrect) Ex – I wish I knew the answer to this question. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Accrue (Verb)
Meaning: (of a sum of money, benefit, or advantage) to accumulate or increase over time; to come to someone as a natural result or growth
Ex – Interest continues to accrue on the outstanding loan amount every month.
Synonyms: accumulate, mount up, amass
Antonyms: diminish, dwindle, decrease
