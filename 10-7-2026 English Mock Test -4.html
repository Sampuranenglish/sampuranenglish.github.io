Rule of the Day
When two subjects are joined by "as well as", "along with", "in addition to", or "together with", the verb agrees only with the first subject, not the one that follows. Ex – The manager, along with his employees, are going to attend the meeting. (Incorrect) Ex – The manager, along with his employees, is going to attend the meeting. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Astute (Adjective)
Meaning: having or showing a sharp ability to assess situations and turn them to one's advantage; shrewd
Ex – She is an astute businesswoman who never misses a good opportunity.
Trick: Astute → sounds like "a-STUTE (student)" who is sharp and quick to understand.
Synonyms: shrewd, sharp, clever, perceptive, canny, sagacious
Antonyms: naive, foolish, gullible, obtuse, unwise
