Rule of the Day
Use the Present Perfect Tense (has/have + V3) for an action that started in the past and is still continuing, or one that took place at an unspecified time in the past but is relevant to the present. Never use the Present Perfect with a definite past time expression (e.g., yesterday, last year, in 2020, ago) — use the Simple Past instead. Ex – I have finished the report yesterday. (Incorrect) Ex – I finished the report yesterday. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Copious (Adjective)
Meaning: in large amounts, or more than enough; abundant; plentiful
Ex – They drank copious amount of wine last night.
Synonyms: abundant, plentiful, ample, profuse
Antonyms: scarce, meagre, sparse
