Rule of the Day
Adjectives beginning with "a-"such as "alone", "afraid", "asleep", "alive", and "awake" are normally used only in the predicative position (after a linking verb like "is/was"), not directly before a noun. Ex- She is an alone girl. (Incorrect) Ex- She is alone in the house. / She is a lonely girl. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Occult (Adjective)
Meaning: relating to mystical, supernatural, or magical beliefs, practices, or phenomena beyond ordinary understanding
Ex \u2013 The old manuscript was filled with occult symbols and mysterious rituals.
Synonyms: mystical, supernatural, esoteric
Antonyms: scientific, rational, mundane
