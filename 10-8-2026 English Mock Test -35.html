Rule of the Day
Never use two modal verbs together, such as "must have to", "can be able to" or "should must" — only one modal auxiliary is permitted before the main verb. This is a frequent SSC CGL error-spotting trap. Ex – She must have to attend the meeting tomorrow. (Incorrect) Ex – She must attend the meeting tomorrow. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Nebulous (Adjective)
Meaning: unclear, vague, or ill-defined; lacking definite form or content
Ex – The startup's long-term strategy remained nebulous even after the investors' meeting.
Synonyms: vague, hazy, indistinct, ambiguous
Antonyms: clear, precise, definite
