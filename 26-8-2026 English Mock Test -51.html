Rule of the Day
When two or more adjectives come before a noun, they follow a fixed order — Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Colour → Origin → Material → Purpose. Stacking them in the wrong order sounds unnatural, even if every word is correct. Ex – She bought a wooden small old table. (Incorrect) Ex – She bought a small old wooden table. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Estranged (Adjective)
Meaning: no longer close or affectionate to someone; alienated
Ex – After years of conflict, he became estranged from his family.
Synonyms: alienated, separated, distant, disaffected
Antonyms: reconciled, united, close
