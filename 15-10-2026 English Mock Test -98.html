Erudite (Adjective)
Meaning: having or showing great knowledge or learning, especially from extensive reading
Ex – The erudite professor delivered a lecture that impressed scholars from many fields.
Synonyms: learned, scholarly, knowledgeable, well-read, lettered
Antonyms: ignorant, illiterate, uneducated, naive, unlearned
