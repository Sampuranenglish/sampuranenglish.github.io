Baffle (Verb)
Meaning: to confuse or puzzle someone completely; to make something hard to understand or solve
Ex – The detective was baffled by the sudden disappearance of the evidence.
Synonyms: puzzle, perplex, bewilder, confuse, confound
Antonyms: enlighten, clarify, explain, illuminate, elucidate
