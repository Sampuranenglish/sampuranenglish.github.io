Rule of the Day
When comparing only two persons or things, use the comparative degree of the adjective followed by "than"; the superlative degree is used only when comparing more than two. Ex – She is the tallest of the two sisters. (Incorrect) Ex – She is the taller of the two sisters. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Circumspect (Adjective)
Meaning: wary and unwilling to take risks; cautious and careful to consider all consequences
Ex – The lawyer advised her client to be circumspect while answering the investigator's questions.
Synonyms: cautious, prudent, wary, discreet
Antonyms: reckless, careless, rash
