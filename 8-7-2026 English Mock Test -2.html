Rule of the Day
After "dare not" and "need not", we use the bare infinitive form of the verb (V1), not the infinitive form (to + V1). Ex – You need not to go there tomorrow. (Incorrect) Ex – You need not go there tomorrow. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Profligate (Adjective)
Meaning: wasting money, time, etc. or using it in a careless way (फ़िज़ूलख़र्च)
Ex – She is well-known for her profligate spending habits.
Trick: Profligate = Profit "gone" → by wasteful spending.
Synonyms: reckless, lavish, extravagant, spendthrift, squandering, improvident
Antonyms: careful, thrifty, economical, frugal, parsimonious, prudent
