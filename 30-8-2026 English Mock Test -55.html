Rule of the Day
Rule 10 (Chapter: Adjective) — “Comparatively” and “Relatively” are always followed by the Positive Degree of the adjective, not the Comparative Degree. Ex – You are comparatively safer here. (Incorrect) Ex – You are comparatively safe here. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Trite (Adjective)
Meaning: overused and unoriginal, lacking freshness due to repeated use
Ex – The speech was full of trite phrases that failed to inspire the audience.
Synonyms: clichéd, hackneyed, banal, stale
Antonyms: original, fresh, novel
