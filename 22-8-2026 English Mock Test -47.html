Rule of the Day
Use "so + adjective + that" when the adjective stands alone, but "such + a/an + adjective + noun + that" when the adjective is followed by a noun. Ex- It was such hot that we couldn't go outside. (Incorrect) Ex- It was so hot that we couldn't go outside. / It was such a hot day that we couldn't go outside. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Vindicate (Verb)
Meaning: to clear someone of blame or suspicion, or to show that something is true, reasonable, or justified
Ex- The court's verdict finally vindicated her long-standing claims of innocence.
Synonyms: exonerate, absolve, acquit, justify
Antonyms: incriminate, accuse, convict, condemn
