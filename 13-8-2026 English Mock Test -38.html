Rule of the Day
"For" is used with a period of time (duration), while "since" is used with a specific point in time. Never interchange the two. Ex – I have been living here since five years. (Incorrect) Ex – I have been living here for five years. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ephemeral (Adjective)
Meaning: lasting for a very short time
Ex – Fame in the digital age can be ephemeral, vanishing as quickly as it arrives.
Synonyms: transient, fleeting, momentary
Antonyms: permanent, enduring, everlasting
