Rule of the Day
Wish + Past Simple
After wish, use the past simple for a present wish. Ex – I wish I know the answer. (Incorrect) Ex – I wish I knew the answer. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Concurrence (Noun)
Meaning: agreement or consent; also, the occurrence of two or more things at the same time
Ex – The merger cannot go ahead without the concurrence of the board.
Synonyms: agreement, consent, approval, assent, accord
Antonyms: disagreement, dissent, opposition, discord
