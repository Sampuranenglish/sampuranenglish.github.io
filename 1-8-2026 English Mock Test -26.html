Rule of the Day
"Affect" is a verb meaning to influence something, while "effect" is usually a noun meaning a result. Do not confuse the two. Ex – The new policy will greatly effect employees. (Incorrect) Ex – The new policy will greatly affect employees. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Formidable (Adjective)
Meaning: inspiring fear or respect through being impressively powerful, intense, or capable
Ex – The champion faced a formidable opponent in the final round of the tournament.
Synonyms: daunting, redoubtable, impressive, intimidating
Antonyms: weak, unimpressive, feeble
