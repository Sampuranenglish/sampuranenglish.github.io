Rule of the Day
The First Conditional (If + present simple, ... will + base verb) is used to talk about real or very likely possibilities in the future — what will happen if a certain condition is fulfilled. Never use ‘will’ in the if-clause. Ex – If it will rain tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic. (Incorrect) Ex – If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Foreboding (Noun / Adjective)
Meaning: a feeling that something bad is going to happen; an ominous warning
Ex – The dark clouds filled her with a sense of foreboding as she set out on the journey.
Synonyms: premonition, dread, apprehension, omen, misgiving
Antonyms: assurance, confidence, optimism, hope
