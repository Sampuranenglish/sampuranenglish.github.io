Rule of the Day
Modal verbs (can, could, may, might, must, shall, should, will, would) are always followed directly by the base form of the verb, never by "to". Ex – You must to complete the task today. (Incorrect) Ex – You must complete the task today. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Sagacious (Adjective)
Meaning: having or showing keen mental discernment and good judgement; wise
Ex – The sagacious old professor was often consulted before any major policy decision.
Synonyms: wise, shrewd, astute, judicious
Antonyms: foolish, imprudent, naive
