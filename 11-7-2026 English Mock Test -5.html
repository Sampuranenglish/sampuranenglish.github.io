Rule of the Day
Uncountable nouns (such as advice, information, furniture, luggage) always take a singular verb and are never used with "a/an" or in the plural form. Ex – He has many furnitures in his house. (Incorrect) Ex – He has a lot of furniture in his house. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ubiquitous (Adjective)
Meaning: present, appearing, or found everywhere
Ex – Smartphones have become ubiquitous in modern society.
Trick: Ubiquitous → sounds like "you-BIK-witus" — like your bike, it seems to be everywhere you look!
Synonyms: omnipresent, pervasive, universal, widespread, everywhere
Antonyms: rare, scarce, uncommon, infrequent, limited
