Rule of the Day
When "either...or" or "neither...nor" joins two subjects, the verb agrees with the subject nearer to it (the second one), not necessarily the first. Ex – Either the manager or his assistants was informed about the delay. (Incorrect) Ex – Either the manager or his assistants were informed about the delay. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ameliorate (Verb)
Meaning: to make a bad or unsatisfactory situation better
Ex – The new policies were introduced to ameliorate the living conditions of slum dwellers.
Synonyms: improve, enhance, alleviate, upgrade
Antonyms: worsen, aggravate, exacerbate
