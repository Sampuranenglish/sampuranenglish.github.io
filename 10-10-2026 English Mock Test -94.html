Word of the Day
Collate (Verb)
Meaning: to collect and combine information or texts from different sources; to compare things carefully in order to note the differences
Ex – The researcher spent weeks to collate data from several surveys into a single report.
Synonyms: gather, assemble, collect, compare, compile
Antonyms: scatter, separate, distribute, disperse
