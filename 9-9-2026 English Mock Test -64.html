Rule of the Day
(Chapter: Preposition) — "Since" is used with a point of time (a specific date, day, year, or moment) and takes the Present Perfect/Perfect Continuous tense. "For" is used with a period/duration of time. Ex – He has been living in Delhi for 2015. (Incorrect) Ex – He has been living in Delhi since 2015. (Correct) Ex – He has been living in Delhi for ten years. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Perfidious (Adjective)
Meaning: deceitful and untrustworthy; disloyal or treacherous
Ex – The general's perfidious ally secretly negotiated with the enemy even while pledging support on the battlefield.
Synonyms: treacherous, disloyal, deceitful
Antonyms: loyal, faithful, trustworthy
