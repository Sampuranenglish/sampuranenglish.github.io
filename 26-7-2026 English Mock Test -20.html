Rule of the Day
In third conditional sentences, use "If + subject + had + V3" in the if-clause, not "would have + V3". Ex – If I would have known, I would have come. (Incorrect) Ex – If I had known, I would have come. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ameliorate (Verb)
Meaning: to make something bad or unsatisfactory better
Ex – The new drainage system helped ameliorate the flooding problem in the city.
Synonyms: improve, enhance, better, upgrade
Antonyms: worsen, aggravate, deteriorate
