Rule of the Day
Possessive pronouns (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs) already show possession and must never take an apostrophe — unlike possessive nouns (e.g., John's), which do. Ex – This book is her's. (Incorrect) Ex – This book is hers. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Flutter (Verb)
Meaning: to move with quick, light, flapping or wavering movements
Ex – The butterfly fluttered gently from flower to flower in the garden.
Synonyms: flap, quiver, flit, waver
Antonyms: stay still, remain steady
