Rule of the Day
"Each other" is used for two people or things, while "one another" is used for more than two. This distinction is a classic SSC CGL pronoun error-spotting trap. Ex – The three friends greeted each other warmly. (Incorrect) Ex – The three friends greeted one another warmly. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Dexterous (Adjective)
Meaning: showing great skill, especially with the hands; mentally or physically adroit — frequently tested as a synonym/antonym item in SSC CGL Tier-I vocabulary sections
Ex – The surgeon's dexterous hands completed the delicate operation without a single error.
Synonyms: skilful, adroit, deft
Antonyms: clumsy, awkward, maladroit
