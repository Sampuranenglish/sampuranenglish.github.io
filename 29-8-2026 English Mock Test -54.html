Rule of the Day
Degree of Adjective Rule: When two persons/things are compared, use the Comparative degree with "of the two" — never the Superlative degree with "of the two". Ex – He is the tallest of the two brothers. (Incorrect) Ex – He is the taller of the two brothers. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ostracise (Verb)
Meaning: to exclude someone from a society or group; to refuse to associate with or speak to someone
Ex – After the scandal came to light, he was ostracised by his own colleagues.
Synonyms: exclude, banish, shun, blacklist
Antonyms: welcome, include, embrace
