Rule of the Day
Since "each", "every", "either", and "neither" are singular in meaning, they must always be followed by singular verbs and singular pronouns. Ex – Each of the players have brought their own equipment. (Incorrect) Ex – Each of the players has brought his own equipment. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ostentatious (Adjective)
Meaning: characterized by vulgar or pretentious display; designed to impress
Ex – The couple's ostentatious wedding was the talk of the town for months.
Trick: Ostentatious → sounds like "ostent" (extent) → showing off to a great "extent".
Synonyms: showy, flashy, pretentious, flamboyant, gaudy
Antonyms: modest, understated, simple, humble
