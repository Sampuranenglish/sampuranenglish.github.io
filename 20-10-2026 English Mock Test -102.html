Scintillating (Adjective)
Meaning: sparkling or shining brightly; (of a person or talk) brilliantly clever, lively and witty
Ex – Her scintillating speech kept the audience hooked till the very end.
Synonyms: sparkling, dazzling, brilliant, glittering, coruscating
Antonyms: dull, boring, lifeless, drab, insipid
