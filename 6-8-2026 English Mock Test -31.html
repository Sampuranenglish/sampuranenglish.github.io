Rule of the Day
The conjunction "Lest" always expresses a negative purpose and must be followed by "should" + base verb — never by "will not" or "won't", since "lest" itself already carries the meaning of "not". Ex – He worked hard lest he will fail in the exam. (Incorrect) Ex – He worked hard lest he should fail in the exam. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ubiquitous (Adjective)
Meaning: present, appearing, or found everywhere
Ex – Smartphones have become so ubiquitous that it is hard to imagine daily life without one.
Synonyms: omnipresent, pervasive, universal, widespread
Antonyms: rare, scarce, uncommon
