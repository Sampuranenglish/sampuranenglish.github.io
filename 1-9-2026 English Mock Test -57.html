Rule of the Day
(Chapter: Verb) — If a pronoun is used before a gerund (V1 + ing), we use the possessive case of that pronoun (my, our, your, his, her, their, its). Ex – I appreciated them coming early. (Incorrect) Ex – I appreciated their coming early. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Promiscuous (Adjective)
Meaning: characterized by a casual, indiscriminate mixing or mingling — showing little care, selection, or discrimination (also used to describe having numerous casual relationships)
Ex – The critic complained about the anthology's promiscuous blending of genres, styles, and eras.
Synonyms: indiscriminate, haphazard, undiscriminating
Antonyms: selective, discriminating, particular
