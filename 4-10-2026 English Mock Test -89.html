Rule of the Day
“Comparatively” and “Relatively” are always followed by the Positive Degree of the adjective, not the Comparative Degree. Ex – You are comparatively safer here. (Incorrect) Ex – You are comparatively safe here. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Impeccable (Adjective)
Meaning: (of behaviour, performance or appearance) free from fault or mistake; perfect
Ex – The chef’s impeccable service earned the restaurant a five-star rating.
Synonyms: flawless, faultless, perfect, immaculate, spotless
Antonyms: flawed, faulty, imperfect, blemished, defective
