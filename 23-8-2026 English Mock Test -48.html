Rule of the Day
Pronoun Rule: A pronoun must agree with its antecedent (the noun it replaces) in number and gender. When two or more singular nouns are joined by "or"/"nor", the pronoun used for them must also be singular. Ex – Neither Rita nor Sonia has brought their book. (Incorrect) Ex – Neither Rita nor Sonia has brought her book. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Capitulate (Verb)
Meaning: to cease resisting and accept an opponent's demands; to give up or surrender
Ex – After weeks of protest, the management finally capitulated to the workers' demands.
Synonyms: surrender, submit, yield, succumb
Antonyms: resist, withstand, defy
