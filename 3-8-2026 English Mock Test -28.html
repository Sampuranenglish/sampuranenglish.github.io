Rule of the Day
"Suggest" is followed either by a gerund (suggest doing something) or by "that + subject + base form" (the subjunctive mood), but never by "to + infinitive". Ex – The doctor suggested me to take rest. (Incorrect) Ex – The doctor suggested that I take rest. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Vindicate (Verb)
Meaning: to clear someone of blame or suspicion; to show or prove to be right or justified
Ex – The court's verdict finally vindicated her after years of false accusations.
Synonyms: exonerate, absolve, acquit, justify
Antonyms: incriminate, condemn, convict
