Rule of the Day
The Third Conditional (If + past perfect, ... would have + V3) is used to talk about unreal or imaginary situations in the past — things that did not happen, and their imagined results. It expresses regret, criticism or a different outcome. Never use ‘would have’ in the if-clause. Ex – If she would have studied harder, she would have passed the exam. (Incorrect) Ex – If she had studied harder, she would have passed the exam. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Commotion (Noun)
Meaning: a state of noisy confusion and disturbance; a sudden loud and excited disorder
Ex – There was a commotion outside the shop when the crowd rushed in for the sale.
Synonyms: uproar, disturbance, turmoil, tumult, fuss
Antonyms: calm, peace, tranquillity, order
