Rule of the Day
Pronoun Rule (Reflexive Pronouns): A reflexive pronoun ("myself," "himself," "herself," "themselves," etc.) is used only when the subject and the object of the verb refer to the same person. It should never be used in place of a simple personal pronoun ("I," "he," "they") as the subject of a sentence. Ex- Myself and my friend went to the market. (Incorrect) Ex- My friend and I went to the market. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Decimate (Verb)
Meaning: to destroy or kill a large proportion of something
Ex – The forest fire threatened to decimate the local wildlife population within a matter of days.
Synonyms: devastated, annihilated, ravaged, wiped out
Antonyms: restored, rebuilt, preserved
