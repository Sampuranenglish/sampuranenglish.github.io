Rule of the Day
"Its" is a possessive pronoun (no apostrophe), while "it's" is a contraction of "it is" or "it has". Never confuse the two. Ex – The company announced it's quarterly results. (Incorrect) Ex – The company announced its quarterly results. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Ostensible (Adjective)
Meaning: appearing or claiming to be true, but not necessarily so
Ex – The ostensible reason for his resignation was health, though colleagues suspected otherwise.
Synonyms: apparent, seeming, professed, alleged
Antonyms: genuine, real, actual
