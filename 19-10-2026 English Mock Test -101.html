Impetuous (Adjective)
Meaning: acting or done quickly and without thinking carefully; rash and impulsive
Ex – His impetuous decision to quit the job left him with no savings.
Synonyms: rash, hasty, impulsive, reckless, precipitate
Antonyms: cautious, careful, wary, prudent, circumspect
