Rule of the Day
(Chapter: Determiners) — Negative sentences take 'any', not 'some'. Ex – They hardly had some information about the seminar. (Incorrect) Ex – They hardly had any information about the seminar. (Correct)
Note: "Hardly" means almost none or almost not.
Word of the Day
Surmount (Verb)
Meaning: to overcome a difficulty or obstacle
Ex – With sheer determination, she was able to surmount every obstacle in her path to success.
Synonyms: overcome, conquer, prevail over
Antonyms: surrender, yield, succumb
