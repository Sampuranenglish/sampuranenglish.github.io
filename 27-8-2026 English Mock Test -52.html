Rule of the Day
The correct usage are: "Enough + Noun" and "Adjective + Enough". Ex- I have enough money. (Correct) money= noun Ex- He is smart enough to solve this question. (Correct) smart = adjective
Word of the Day
Regress (Verb)
Meaning: to return to a former, typically worse or less developed, state; to move backward instead of forward
Ex- Without regular practice, his spoken English began to regress within a few months.
Synonyms: revert, relapse, decline, backslide
Antonyms: progress, advance, improve
