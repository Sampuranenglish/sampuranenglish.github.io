Rule of the Day
Use "between" when referring to exactly two people or things, and "among" when referring to more than two. Ex – Divide the profits between the four partners. (Incorrect) Ex – Divide the profits among the four partners. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Assiduous (Adjective)
Meaning: showing great care, attention and effort; diligent and persistent
Ex – Her assiduous preparation over many months finally paid off in the final exam.
Synonyms: diligent, industrious, painstaking, sedulous
Antonyms: lazy, negligent, indolent
