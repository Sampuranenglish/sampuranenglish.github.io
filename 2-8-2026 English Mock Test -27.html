Rule of the Day
"Used to + base verb" describes a past habit that no longer happens. Do not confuse it with "use to" (a common misspelling) or "be used to + gerund" (meaning accustomed to something). Ex – I use to go for a walk every morning as a child. (Incorrect) Ex – I used to go for a walk every morning as a child. (Correct)
Word of the Day
Pertinent (Adjective)
Meaning: relevant or applicable to a particular matter
Ex – The lawyer raised several pertinent questions during the cross-examination.
Synonyms: relevant, applicable, apt, apposite
Antonyms: irrelevant, unrelated, extraneous
